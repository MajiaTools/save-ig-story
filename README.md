## What it does

- **Stories & Highlights** — save an active story before it expires, or come back to a saved highlight anytime
- **Reels, photos, videos** — dedicated tools for Reels, feed photos, video posts, and profile pictures
- **Original quality** — JPG/PNG for images, MP4 for videos; no extra compression, no added watermark or logo overlay
- **No account needed** — a public link is all you need; keep your Instagram password to yourself; Instagram sends no notification
- **Nothing to install** — works in any browser on phone or desktop; files save to your gallery or Downloads folder
- **Completely free** — no subscriptions, no hidden fees, no download limits; files are processed in real time and never saved on our servers

## How to use

1. Copy the public story or highlight link
2. Open https://saveigstory.com and paste the link into the box
3. Tap Download and pick your quality
